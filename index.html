Taraba NEXTGEN Youth Stakeholders Conference 2027

Grow Here. Build Here. Prosper Here.

Youth and Women Transforming Agriculture into Jobs, Enterprise and Climate Solutions

24–26 June 2027
Jalingo, Taraba State, Nigeria
150 Young Delegates · Ages 18–35
Start Application →Relive NEXTGEN 2026
“What if the greener pasture young people are searching for is already growing beneath their feet?”

Grow here. Build here. Prosper here.

The conference will bring together 150 young people aged 18–35 from the 16 LGAs of Taraba State, other parts of Nigeria and internationally to explore how agriculture can become a stronger pathway to jobs, entrepreneurship, investment, women's economic empowerment and climate resilience.
